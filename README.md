**Bone Marrow Transplant for Adults Over 60 in India**

Bone Marrow Transplant (BMT), also known as Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation (HSCT), is an advanced medical procedure used to treat certain blood cancers, bone marrow disorders, and immune system conditions. For adults over 60, a bone marrow transplant may be considered based on their overall health, medical history, disease condition, and ability to tolerate the treatment.

**Is Bone Marrow Transplant Possible After 60?**

Yes, selected adults over 60 may be eligible for a bone marrow transplant. Age alone does not determine transplant eligibility. Doctors evaluate the patient's physical fitness, heart and lung function, kidney and liver health, disease status, and other medical conditions before recommending treatment.

**Types of Bone Marrow Transplant for Older Adults**

Depending on the patient's diagnosis and medical condition, specialists may recommend:

Autologous Transplant: Uses the patient's own previously collected stem cells.

Allogeneic Transplant: Uses healthy stem cells from a suitable donor.

Reduced-Intensity Conditioning (RIC): Uses lower doses of chemotherapy, with or without radiation, before an allogeneic transplant.

Haploidentical Transplant: Uses stem cells from a partially matched family donor.

**Conditions That May Require BMT**

Bone marrow transplantation may be considered for selected patients with:

Leukemia

Lymphoma

Multiple Myeloma

Myelodysplastic Syndromes (MDS)

Aplastic Anaemia

Certain blood and immune system disorders
